      Sleeping Plan for 6 People (+ Festival Overflow)

      3 Quick Fixes from Your Original Excel Sketch

      1. Right-side width (9 ft) is tight for double beds: In your Excel sheet, Left = 11', Corridor = 5', Right = 9'. A 9' wide Bedroom 2 leaves only 2.5 ft walking space at the foot of a 6.5' bed. Widening the right side to 10.5' or 11' (by integrating the corridor into the courtyard/living) makes Room 2 & Dining feel much more spacious.

      2. Split Living vs Unified Front Living + Courtyard: Your Excel has two separate Living zones (`11×16` left + `9×16` right) split by a 5' passage. Merging them around the front Courtyard (Nadumuttam) creates one grand festival gathering hall.

      3. Dedicated Pantry + Store + Bathrooms: Adding an walk-in Pantry next to the Kitchen and ensuring 1 attached bath + 1 common bath makes hosting 6 people effortless in the mornings.

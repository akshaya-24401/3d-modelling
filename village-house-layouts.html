Questions to settle before the next round

  1. Which direction does the road side face? If you are following vastu, this decides where the pooja, kitchen and main bedroom go, and the options can be mirrored to suit.
  2. Is the plot exactly 25′ × 74′? Check whether the panchayat needs side or rear setbacks. They would make the house narrower than drawn.
  3. Do you want stairs to the terrace? Useful for drying clothes, sleeping out on summer nights, or adding a floor later. Stairs need about 3′ × 12′.
  4. What is Loki's shed for? A pet, cattle and storage each need a different distance from the kitchen and bedrooms.
  5. Festival cooking: indoor kitchen only, or an outdoor wood-fire stove (viragu aduppu) in the service yard for Pongal and big batches?
  6. Where do all 6 sleep? Two bedrooms take 4 comfortably. Are the other 2 fine on mats in the hall or thinnai, or should the hall have a cot nook?

These are zoning sketches for brainstorming. Walls are drawn as single lines, so real rooms come out about 9″ smaller each way. Doors, windows, structure and drainage still need an architect or civil engineer.
